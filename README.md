# Privacy Policy — Seher

## Overview

Seher has no accounts, advertising or analytics SDKs, and no server of its own. Prayer times and related alarms require a confirmed calendar download first; saved dates work offline. Included duas, salawat, their translations and explanations, and local records are available offline without contacting their sources. Calendar and recitation downloads, location services, and voluntary sharing are described below. The included Muhammet Abay and Tanzil texts are stored on the device. Reading or switching these editions sends no data to their providers. The Cuz and Hizb index is also bundled locally; opening reading sections sends no data to Diyanet. Initial reading marks in duas are also applied on the device; no text or reading information is transmitted for this. The included German, English, Turkish and Chinese Quran meaning translations and their source notes are stored on the device and read offline. Selecting a translation or changing text size sends no data to Tanzil or the translators. The translation choice and whether it is shown are saved locally for each app language. Opening a source link is optional and uses your browser. Al-Razi’s Arabic Quran commentary is also included on the device and can be read offline. Opening its sections or introductions requires no download, contacts no provider and adds no tracking. The app language also determines which Quran translation and commentary are offered in reading and search. This filtering happens entirely on the device. Existing references are retained locally; no text is translated by an online service. Ma Yulong’s Chinese Quran commentary and its complete preface are also bundled for offline reading. Opening them sends no requests to chinese-tafsir.com or the Basayir waqf. The Chinese commentary is offered in Chinese; Razi’s commentary is offered in Arabic. Local test editions may include Bilmen’s Turkish commentary, in the Kitap Kalbi 2020 edition prepared by Hüsamettin Vanlıoğlu and Fatih Kalender. Permission to redistribute this edited edition has not yet been documented. Reading it requires no network download, contacts no source provider and collects no additional personal data. Local test editions may also include the complete English Tafsir al-Jalalayn translated by Feras Hamza. The text, search index and reading references are stored locally. Reading contacts neither Altafsir.com nor its publisher and sends no reading data. Permission to redistribute the English translation has not yet been documented.

## Location

The bundled GeoNames city names and search aliases are searched only on your device. Search terms are not sent to GeoNames or another service. City search and manual coordinates work offline. With location permission, Seher can request your position through Google Play services while the app is open, including checking or updating it when you return. This can use GPS, Wi-Fi or mobile networks depending on device settings; Google location services have their own data processing and privacy rules. You can use city search or coordinates without granting this permission. Seher stores the chosen position locally for prayer times and Qibla and does not include it in recitation requests. Compass sensor readings are processed locally and are not stored or uploaded by Seher. An earlier pending location request cannot replace a newer city or coordinate selection. During an automatic location check, Seher keeps the saved GPS position if it is still within the uncertainty radius reported for the new fix. An explicit request to use your current location remains available. This comparison happens only on your device. When GPS changes, Seher may reuse an already confirmed Diyanet or Semerkand city calendar within the same locally identified city. Offline place identifiers, the approved calendar city and coordinates are compared on your device. Postal codes are not collected and this comparison alone does not download new prayer times. Neighboring cities and coordinate-specific AlAdhan calendars are not treated as equivalent. To label a GPS position, Seher also asks the Android place-name service (Geocoder). Depending on the device, this may send coordinates and the requested language to the device's geocoding provider over the Internet. That provider has its own data processing and privacy rules. Seher does not operate this service or a server of its own. The app retains only the selected coordinates, city and optional district, location accuracy and naming source; it does not retain street addresses or postal codes returned by the service. If the service is unavailable, an offline nearest-place label is shown as approximate. A location you explicitly request must be fresh; uncertain results require your confirmation. When location services are switched off, Seher offers to open Android's activation dialog or settings. Changing only a place label does not authorize another prayer-calendar download.

## Notifications & alarms

Notification and exact-alarm permissions support prayer notifications, reminders and wake-up alarms. The optional prayer focus uses Do Not Disturb access to manage its temporary quiet period. This does not give Seher access to the contents of other apps’ notifications. Whether Seher has already requested notification permission is stored only on this device, so a denied request can lead to Android settings. This device-specific flag is not included in manual backups.

## What's stored on your device

Seher stores your selected location, calculation and alarm settings, appearance preferences, Quran bookmarks and reading progress locally. Optional Tahajjud, fasting and daily self-reckoning records, including notes, and tasbih and missed-prayer counters also stay in local storage. They are not automatically uploaded. Android backup and device-transfer rules exclude the settings store, practice database and downloaded recitations; system-managed settings may follow Android’s own rules. A small local alarm diagnostic keeps the last execution result and timestamps for each alarm task. It contains no location, prayer times, notes or exception messages, is excluded from backups and is never automatically sent. Pending offline audio requests are also stored locally to explain interruptions. This download journal is excluded from exported backups. Reading text sizes are stored only on your device. Duas and Dala’il al-Khayrat share one text size; the Quran has a separate setting. Hadiths use the same locally saved reading size as duas and Dalāʾil. The selected prayer, reading date, current step and repetition counters for tesbihat are also stored only on the device. The tesbihat text compared with the book is available offline; reading it does not contact the author or publisher. The last Dala’il al-Khayrat section, paragraph and scroll position are also saved locally so you can continue reading. No reading position is sent to a text provider. A pending Tahajjud snooze time is stored only on this device so that alarm rescheduling and a restart do not discard it. It is removed when used, stopped, disabled or expired. This temporary alarm state is excluded from manual backups, Android backup and device transfer. Reading positions also store stable chapter and passage identifiers, the content revision and reading-layout information such as language, text size and screen dimensions locally. Old positions that cannot be matched reliably are retained locally for clarification. These reading details, including retained unresolved positions, are included in manual backups. They are not sent over the network. Text search processes only bundled texts on your device. The smaller text index is built in memory when needed. The commentary uses a separate search index stored only on this device, without search history; this reproducible index is excluded from backups. Seher keeps no permanent search history and sends neither search terms nor results to providers. The query, scope and result-list position are kept for returning to the search and may be saved temporarily by Android to restore the screen. Calendar download jobs and their place lists, progress and limited confirmations also remain only on this device and are excluded from backups and device transfer. Personal collections, their names, ordered text references and separate reading sessions are stored only on this device. They are not sent to content providers. Deleting a collection also deletes its session, without deleting original texts or favorites. Bookmarks for Quran translations and commentary, and collection reading positions, store the exact edition and passage locally. Manual backups include these references, not copies of the source texts or the search index. If an edition is missing, Seher keeps the reference and does not silently open another text. The page index and prostration metadata are bundled locally. Opening pages or showing these reading aids sends no requests to Diyanet, TDV or the text author. Seher stores only on this device whether you completed or skipped the introduction. You can replay it from Settings.

## Prayer calendar downloads

Seher may automatically offer a calendar download when your location or method changes. Only after you confirm the download does Seher connect over HTTPS to namazvakitleri.diyanet.gov.tr (Diyanet), semerkandtakvimi.com (Semerkand) or api.aladhan.com (other supported methods). The provider receives your IP address, requested places and dates, and a fixed app label. AlAdhan also receives the selected coordinates, which can be precise GPS coordinates, time zone and calculation options. Diyanet and Semerkand do not receive your selected GPS coordinates. No practice records, notes, accounts or individual device identifiers are sent. After confirmation, Seher retrieves the provider’s place directory if needed and automatically finds the matching calendar location. Matching happens on your device. If the provider does not list your place, Seher may use a clearly identified nearby calendar location in the same country and time zone. This comparison uses the bundled city list on your device. The actual provider location remains visible; nearby times are not calculated for your exact position. Existing matching calendars can be reused offline without a new download. Declining the offer starts no calendar request. Country and multi-place downloads remain separate choices in saved places. Country downloads cover only listed places and published dates; AlAdhan uses the catalogue cities’ coordinates. At most three requests run concurrently; the separate place-browsing cache is kept in memory for up to 24 hours. Completed calendars are saved in small batches. Stopping retains completed places; repeating a country skips current calendars. Choosing a country does not download your saved places in other countries. Refreshing all saved places is a separate confirmed action. Calendars and place assignments stay in private device storage until you delete them individually or together. Deleting all calendars preserves your selected location, settings and practice records. Calendars and reminder choices are excluded from Android backups, device transfers and manual backups. Local checks may remind you after three months, when fewer than seven days remain or the place/source changes; they make no network requests. Each new download needs your confirmation. Missing days have no replacement calculation or dependent prayer alarms. Viewing saved places starts no new download. Previously confirmed jobs may continue within their stated permission. Provider logs and retention are controlled by the providers; verified retention periods are not available to us and Seher cannot delete their logs. Saved places show uninterrupted coverage from today. Later saved periods and missing days are listed separately. Saved AlAdhan calendars for different Asr and high-latitude options remain separate and can be reused offline when you return to those settings. An update reminder does not delete stored prayer times. If a new download includes today and tomorrow, automatic download prompts pause until the next local day, even if the provider supplies fewer than seven days. Manual downloads remain available. Download jobs, selected places and provider options, progress in finding places, results, error categories and time-limited confirmations are also stored privately on this device. A confirmation covers only the original scope and source for at most 24 hours. The same job may continue after closing the view or a brief system interruption; after a device restart, expiry or uncertain validity, continuing requires another explicit confirmation. Pause and cancel stop automatic continuation. Force stop is respected. Temporary network errors may receive limited retries; access restrictions (403/429) stop automatic attempts. Saved calendars remain available. Deleting calendars stops related work so it cannot recreate deleted data. Jobs and network permissions are excluded from manual backups, Android backups and device transfer; restoring a backup never starts an imported download. No additional provider, account or tracking service is introduced.

## Quran recitation audio

When you start recitation playback or a download, missing verses are fetched over HTTPS from EveryAyah (everyayah.com), which uses BunnyCDN (bunny.net) to deliver the files. The delivery service receives your IP address, the requested reciter, sura and verse, and a fixed Seher/Android application label. BunnyCDN derives an approximate country from your IP address. Seher does not add an individual user or device identifier, your saved prayer location or GPS coordinates, notes or account data to these requests. Playback can load upcoming verses in advance and continue while the app is in the background. You can use Seher without online recitation playback or downloads. Saved verses play offline and remain until removed through the app or Android app-data controls. We have no verified information about EveryAyah’s specific request-log settings, retention periods or additional uses of request data and cannot promise that no request data is stored. Changing the reciter during playback can fetch the current verse and upcoming verses for the new reciter from the same service. Reciter and translation display preferences are stored only on your device.

## Backup files you create

A manual backup is an unencrypted JSON text file containing your saved location, settings, reading state, counters and practice records, including personal notes. Downloaded audio, prayer calendars and device-local calendar reminder choices are not included. After restoring a backup, prayer times require a new confirmed calendar download. You select a file provider and destination in Android; that provider receives the file and may upload it if you choose a cloud location. Seher has no automatic cloud sync. Keep the file private and delete unneeded copies at their destination. The saved Dala’il al-Khayrat section, paragraph and scroll position are included in this manual backup. Download jobs and their confirmations are not imported; restoring a backup stops existing calendar download work. Manual backups also contain collections and their reading sessions. Restoring an older backup without collections preserves existing collections. A backup in the new format replaces them with its collection data, including an explicitly empty list. Bookmarks for Quran translations and commentary, and collection reading positions, store the exact edition and passage locally. Manual backups include these references, not copies of the source texts or the search index. If an edition is missing, Seher keeps the reference and does not silently open another text.

## Data sharing

Sharing a Ramadan timetable gives the selected app an image containing the displayed place and prayer times. Optional Quran and translation source links open tanzil.net in your browser; tafsir source links open quranpedia.net, chinese-tafsir.com or altafsir.com, depending on the included edition. The receiving app, browser and website apply their own privacy rules. These actions are optional. Seher does not sell your data or send your practice records to an analytics service.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you change or delete them, clear Seher’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local app data, including downloaded audio. Exported backups and shared copies must be deleted separately in the chosen service or app. Seher cannot delete request logs held by an external provider.

Privacy Policy — Seher
Last updated: July 2026

Seher is a prayer-times and Islamic-practice app for Android. This policy explains what the app does, and does not do with your data.

Summary
Seher does not have user accounts, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not run its own server. Everything the app calculates or remembers is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Location
Seher uses your location only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. You can provide it in one of three ways:

searching for a city from a bundled offline list,
entering coordinates manually, or
using your device's GPS (requested only while the app is open in the foreground. Never in the background).
Whichever method you choose, your location is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Device sensors (compass)
The Qibla compass feature reads your device's motion and magnetic-field sensors to show the direction of the Kaaba. This data is processed on your device in real time to draw the compass needle and is never stored or transmitted anywhere.

Notifications & alarms
Seher asks for the notification permission to show prayer-time and reminder notifications, and for the exact-alarm permission so those notifications and the Azan sound fire precisely on time rather than being delayed by the system.

What's stored on your device
Your location, calculation settings, chosen design/theme/language, and alarm preferences are stored locally. If you turn on the optional Tahajjud (night prayer) tracking feature, your tracked nights are stored locally as well. None of this is uploaded anywhere, and none of it is included in Android's own device backup either.

Data sharing
Seher does not share any data with third parties, because it does not send any data anywhere in the first place.

Children's privacy
Seher is a general-purpose religious-practice utility and is not directed at young children. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, regardless of age. See "Summary" above.

Changes to this policy
If this policy changes in a future update, the updated version will be posted at this same page and reflected in the app's own copy under Settings → About → Privacy policy.

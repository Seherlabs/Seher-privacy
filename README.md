# Privacy Policy — Seher

## Overview

Seher has no accounts, advertising or analytics SDKs, and no server of its own. Prayer times, alarms, calendars and bundled texts work offline. Optional recitation downloads, device location services and the files or links you choose to open or share are described below.

## Location

City search and manual coordinates work offline. With location permission, Seher can request your position through Google Play services while the app is open, including checking or updating it when you return. This can use GPS, Wi-Fi or mobile networks depending on device settings; Google location services have their own data processing and privacy rules. You can use city search or coordinates without granting this permission. Seher stores the chosen position locally for prayer times and Qibla and does not include it in recitation requests. Compass sensor readings are processed locally and are not stored or uploaded by Seher.

## Notifications & alarms

Notification and exact-alarm permissions support prayer notifications, reminders and wake-up alarms. The optional prayer focus uses Do Not Disturb access to manage its temporary quiet period. This does not give Seher access to the contents of other apps’ notifications.

## What's stored on your device

Seher stores your selected location, calculation and alarm settings, appearance preferences, Quran bookmarks and reading progress locally. Optional Tahajjud, fasting and daily self-reckoning records, including notes, and tasbih and missed-prayer counters also stay in local storage. They are not automatically uploaded. Android backup and device-transfer rules exclude the settings store, practice database and downloaded recitations; system-managed settings may follow Android’s own rules.

## Quran recitation audio

When you start recitation playback or a download, missing verses are fetched over HTTPS from EveryAyah (everyayah.com), which uses BunnyCDN (bunny.net) to deliver the files. The delivery service receives your IP address, the requested reciter, sura and verse, and a fixed Seher/Android application label. BunnyCDN derives an approximate country from your IP address. Seher does not add an individual user or device identifier, your saved prayer location or GPS coordinates, notes or account data to these requests. Playback can load upcoming verses in advance and continue while the app is in the background. You can use Seher without online recitation playback or downloads. Saved verses play offline and remain until removed through the app or Android app-data controls. We have no verified information about EveryAyah’s specific request-log settings, retention periods or additional uses of request data and cannot promise that no request data is stored.

## Backup files you create

A manual backup is an unencrypted JSON text file containing your saved location, settings, reading state, counters and practice records, including personal notes. Downloaded audio is not included. You select a file provider and destination in Android; that provider receives the file and may upload it if you choose a cloud location. Seher has no automatic cloud sync. Keep the file private and delete unneeded copies at their destination.

## Data sharing

Sharing a Ramadan timetable gives the selected app an image containing the displayed place and prayer times. Opening the Quran source link opens tanzil.net in your browser. The receiving app, browser and website apply their own privacy rules. These actions are optional. Seher does not sell your data or send your practice records to an analytics service.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you change or delete them, clear Seher’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local app data, including downloaded audio. Exported backups and shared copies must be deleted separately in the chosen service or app. Seher cannot delete request logs held by an external provider.

## Changes to this policy

Version: 11 September 2026. Updates to this policy are included in the app and published on the Seher privacy-policy page.
